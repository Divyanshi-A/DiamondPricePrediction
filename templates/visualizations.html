Understanding the Metrics

MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average absolute difference between predicted and actual prices. Lower is better.

RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Root of the average squared differences. More sensitive to large errors. Lower is better.

R² (R-squared): Proportion of variance explained by the model. Closer to 1.0 is better.

Based on these metrics, the Random Forest model performs best overall and was selected for this application.
