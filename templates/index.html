Characteristics Guide

  • Carat: Weight of the diamond (0.2-5.0)
  • Cut: Quality of the cut (Fair to Ideal)
  • Color: Diamond color grade (D to J)
  • Clarity: How clear the diamond is (I1 to IF)
  • Depth: Total depth percentage (43-79)
  • Table: Width of top relative to widest point (43-95)
  • x, y, z: Diamond dimensions in mm
